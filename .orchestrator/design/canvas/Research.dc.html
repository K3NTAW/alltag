Research · what we took from Mobbin and why
12 screens. Each card says what the pattern gave us and which board carries it. Nothing is copied, the patterns are reset into our tokens.
01 · Camera
CameraIdle
CapWords camera
Bracket corners, the line “Curious how to say this in French? Tap to snap”, and a soft shutter. We kept the brackets and the calm prompt, dropped the card stack.

02 · Camera
CameraScanning
Lifesum “Snap a meal”
Full-bleed photo with a dark bottom sheet: eyebrow, headline, shutter. We use the sheet for the looking state, with sun brackets while Gemini works.
View on Mobbin
03 · Roleplay
RoleplayStart
Speak roleplay
Task pill at the top, tutor line in a glass bubble with replay and translate, a “Tap to respond” mic. Ours sits over the live camera image instead of a flat ground.
View on Mobbin
04 · Controls
Control row
Tolan
Camera, chat and mic as one control row under a character. We keep the single row, lose the character: the scene is the character.
View on Mobbin
05 · Voice
RoleplayListening
Claude voice
A single wide pill with a waveform, settings left, close right. Our listening state borrows the waveform and the quiet, one-thing-at-a-time layout.
View on Mobbin
06 · Voice
Thinking state
Pi “is listening”
A calm, text-only state. No spinner, no avatar. Our thinking state copies the restraint: three dots and the words “Noé is thinking”.
View on Mobbin
07 · Corrections
Corrections
Speak correction cards
Orange “what you said”, green “better”, a why, and Retry. We keep the three-part card, swap orange and green for clay and moss, and type each card by error kind.
View on Mobbin
08 · Corrections
Corrections why
Duolingo correction explanation
Strike and replacement, then an examples list. We underline the wrong token instead of striking it and cap the why at two lines.
View on Mobbin
09 · Review
ReviewCard
CapWords card review
Photo card, IPA, “Hold to speak” to master the word. We keep the photo-on-top card and hold-to-speak, and ask for a sentence rather than the word.
View on Mobbin
10 · Review
ReviewResult
Babbel “Did you remember it correctly?”
A No / Yes self-grade after the reveal. We use it as the fallback when speech scoring is unsure: Hard, Okay, Easy under the result.

11 · Onboarding
Onb3Level
Speak level picker
Five levels, each with one line of what you can do. We keep the plain-language lines and add a “Not sure, let Noé find out” row.
