The loop · there are no lessons
1
Look
Camera frame to Gemini. Place, objects, their names at your level, a task that fits the scene.
2
Listen
Noé names three to five things. Gradium voice, French, short sentences.
3
Do
Noé becomes the barista. You hold to talk and get through it. Semantic turn-taking, no button mashing.
4
Fix
After the exchange, not during. What you said, what is better, why. Typed by error kind.
5
Keep
Each fumble becomes a card with the photo. Review by speaking. The camera brings it back when it sees the object again.
