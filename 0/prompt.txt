Fix: translation must never come from the Live model.

2. When a French utterance becomes final, call the existing postTranslate (src/lib/api-listen.ts, POST /api/translate {text, from: "fr", to: "en"}) once for that utterance and set its en when it resolves; mark the pair final then.
